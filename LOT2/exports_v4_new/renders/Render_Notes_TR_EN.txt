The four PDF elevations were visualized with built-in ImageGen using the corresponding v4 SketchUp PNG exports in the same source folder. Files: Front, Left (garage), Rear, Right.
Material source: C:/Projects/JESB/ACC MATERIAL PACKAGE.xlsx, EXTERIOR, Lot 2, B10:B32.

Material regions follow the model exports. Rear and right basement cladding was corrected to white brick, and an invented horizontal opening in the rear rendering was removed.
These are AI visualizations based on the source elevations, not dimensionally authoritative outputs from a SketchUp rendering engine. Textures, colors, fine details and environmental lighting are visual interpretations, not calibrated manufacturer sample matches. Source PDF, SKP and Excel files were not modified.
