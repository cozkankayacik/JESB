Method
Not regenerated with an image model. The driveway outlines were projected from the model plan and composited:
true scale at the wall base, one-point perspective toward the viewer (vanishing point on the image centre line,
190 px above grade, camera 820 in from the wall). Lawn texture is taken from the left render; asphalt tone is
sampled from the previous rear render.

Limits
An elevation sees the ground edge-on, so the foreground is an illustration of the plan, not a measured view.
Positions and widths along the wall base are to scale; depth toward the viewer is foreshortened.
On the front render the driveway is drawn 9 in from the entry steps along its whole width; in the model the lawn strip
between driveway and main wall is 47 in.
The lawn is drawn to the image edges; the model lawn is a finite slab.
