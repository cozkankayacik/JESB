# JESB Lot 20 — Current DWG vs SketchUp Model

Date: 2026-10-07
DWG: `Lot-20 base rend.dwg` (saved 2026-10-07 9:14 AM)
Model: `JESB_LOT20_SKETCHUP_MODEL.skp` (saved 2026-09-09)
Cross-check: `Lot-20-ACC response.pdf` (plotted 2026-10-05, revision "ACC meeting, preliminary items")

Method: the DWG elevations and roof plan were drawn in red over parallel-projection views of the model at the same scale (`JESB_Lot20_Overlay_*.jpg` in this folder). Exterior only. Neither source file was changed.

## Differences (DWG is the reference)

1. **Front elevation, upper wall above the garage.** The model has three small square windows. The DWG has two, each with a sill. The left one is about where the model's left window is, the second is roughly 15" to the right of the model's middle window, and the model's right-hand window is gone. This is the area inside the revision cloud on sheet 1 of the 10/5 PDF.
2. **Siding type.** The model uses horizontal lap siding. The DWG calls for vertical board and batten siding everywhere siding occurs: rear elevation (left bay, balcony bay, garage wing), left side elevation and the rear part of the right side elevation.
3. **Left side elevation, three windows.** The upper picture window, the upper paired casement and the first lower small window next to the main block sit about 12" further toward the rear in the DWG. Sizes match. The other windows on this side match.
4. **Roof heights.** Eave level matches (about 257" above the slab). The DWG main ridge is at 381.6" against 374.0" in the model (7.6" higher). The front hip of the garage roof peaks at 201" in the DWG against 187" in the model (14" higher); the DWG draws those garage hips at 4:12 and the main roof at 6:12.
5. **Roof vents.** The DWG roof plan shows four small roof vents near the main ridge. The model has none.
6. **Fascia and gutters.** The DWG draws an 8" fascia and gutters at every eave (aluminum gutters and downspouts are noted). The model's roof has plain edges.
7. **Basement and grade.** The DWG shows a full basement under the main house with four 48" x 48" escape windows in wells (front right, rear left, left side, right side) and a grade line. The model stops at the slab and has no ground.

## To verify

8. **Balcony / covered patio depth.** On the left side elevation the DWG balcony projects roughly 15"–20" further than the model; on the right side elevation the two agree. One of the two DWG side elevations may be off, so this needs a dimension from the plan.
9. **Garage door style.** Openings match in size and position (16' and 9' doors on the right side, the 9' door on the angled wall, 9' door at the rear). The DWG draws them with a glazed panel grid, which may be only the drafting symbol. The model has solid black doors, and the ACC material list for Lot 20 says "long raised without glass".

## What matches

- Footprint and exterior wall positions on all four sides.
- All other window and door openings: front, rear, right side and the rest of the left side.
- Front elevation materials: brick, stucco bays, stone entry surround.
- Brick piers on the rear elevation, the balcony and its railing, the stone corner on the right side.
- Roof layout in plan, and eave heights.

## Notes

- The DWG contains no revision clouds or dimensions (it is a cleaned "base rend" drawing). Its geometry agrees with the 10/5 PDF, including the two-window revision.
- Interior layout and floor plans were not compared.
- The Lot 20 column of `ACC MATERIAL PACKAGE.xlsx` lists siding as White with a Black "siding dec", which was not checked against the model's colours here.
