## Change: entry tower above the canopy (red-marked areas)

In the DWG the stone piers of the entry tower run straight to the grey surround of the upper window. In the model each pier stopped about 13" short, leaving a recessed stone strip with a dark slot between the pier and the window surround.

- Above the entry canopy (z 194.2" up to the tower frieze, 298.1") both pier fronts were extended to the window surround: left pier to x 1558.4, right pier to x 1637.6. Each pier now reads as one stone face from the tower corner to the window, as drawn.
- Below the canopy nothing was changed (the porch columns stand in front there).
- The stone texture was re-aligned so the extension continues the pier's stone pattern without a seam.

Nothing else was changed.
