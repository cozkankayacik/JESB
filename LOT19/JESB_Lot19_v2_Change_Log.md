# JESB Lot 19 — SketchUp Model v2 Change Log

## 1. Entry tower roof (front, red mark)

In v1 the eave and white fascia of the main roof ran straight across the face of the entry tower, below the tower's own roof. On A-300 the main eave line stops at the tower walls and the tower walls run up to the tower roof.

- The main roof was rebuilt with a notch at the tower: it now stops at the tower's side walls (x 1517.6 / 1678.3) up to the back of the tower frieze. The main fascia ends flush against the tower walls on both sides.
- The tower's hip roof itself (eave 310.2", ridge 356.2", standing-seam metal) already matched the drawing and is unchanged.

## 2. Garage roof (front, red mark)

The first-floor roof over the garage was lower and shaped differently from the drawing (eave 127.2", top 176.6"). Rebuilt as `Roof - Garage (per Rev 1 elevations, v2)` from A-300 (front and left) and A-310 (right):

- Eave top 132.5", 7.3" fascia; side slopes 4:12, front (south) ends 6:12, as drawn.
- Main hip: eaves x 1170–1466, south eave y 975.8, ridge 181.8" from y 1074.5 north to the house.
- Smaller front hip on the west part: eaves x 1170–1324, south eave y 963.1, top 158.2".
- The front elevation now shows the large hip with the smaller hip in front of it on the left, as on the drawing.

## 3. Window above the garage (right elevation)

A window was added on the east wall of the two-storey bay above the garage: the same two-panel casement unit (2 × 3'-0" × 5'-0") as on the west wall of that bay, mirrored to the east wall at the same height and position.

## 4. Garage doors (right elevation)

From south to north: **single-car door, then double door**, as on A-310 (the model had the double door first).

- Single door 8'-0" × 8'-0" (y 1009.5–1105.5), 2 panel columns.
- Double door 16'-0" × 8'-0" (y 1136.0–1328.0), 4 panel columns.
- Raised panels with a glass top row, wood-tone finish (the material of the previous doors), 4" grey casing.
- The stone/brick pier between the doors was moved to sit between the new doors (y 1105.5–1136.0); the end piers are unchanged.
- Doors are 8'-0" high as on the drawing (the previous doors were about 7'-1").

## Not changed

Everything else is as in v1 (walls, other windows, other roofs, materials, deck, site).

## Notes

- The A-310 sheet sits about 5" higher and 12" further north than the model after alignment (its window heads line up with the model only with that shift), so the door and window positions were set from the model's walls and the drawing's dimensions, not by tracing the sheet.
- The roof plan A-220 still conflicts with the elevations (see the v1 change log).
